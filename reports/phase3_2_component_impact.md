# ENNOVERA PHASE 3.2 — FPL COMPONENT IMPACT

- **Appearance Points:** $2 \cdot P(60+) + 1 \cdot P(1\text{--}59)$ accurately matches official FPL threshold scoring.
- **Clean Sheet Points:** Conditioned on $P(60+)$ preventing non-starters from inflating CS xP.
- **Attacking Points:** Goal/assist exposure correctly scaled by conditional playing time.
