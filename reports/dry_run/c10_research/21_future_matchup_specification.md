# C10-C TEAM TACTICAL STYLE & MATCHUP SPECIFICATION (PROPOSED)

## 1. Scientific Objective
Model non-linear stylistic interactions between opposing tactical systems rather than simple scalar difference $S_A - S_B$.

## 2. Key Dimensions
- High Press vs Buildup Resistance
- High Defensive Line vs Direct / Pace Attack
- Low Block vs Possession Breakdown Efficiency
- Aerial Attack vs Aerial Box Defence

## 3. Governance Rule
- No manual bonuses (e.g. "+10% for attacking clash"). All parameters must be learned from training data.
- Scheduled as Priority 3 in the research roadmap.
