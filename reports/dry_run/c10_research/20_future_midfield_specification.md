# C10-B EXPLICIT MIDFIELD LATENT STATE SPECIFICATION (PROPOSED)

## 1. Scientific Objective
Decompose midfield performance into three orthogonal latent factors:
1. `MID_CONTROL`: Possession dominance under pressure, territorial tilt, and retention efficiency.
2. `MID_PROGRESSION`: Progressive passes, carries into final third, and line-breaking passes.
3. `MID_DISRUPTION`: High turnovers forced, counterpress recoveries, and opposition progression suppression.

## 2. Hard Governance Rule
- DO NOT use goals conceded or clean sheets as primary midfield metrics (these confound GK and defensive line effects).
- Only authenticated event data may be used.
- Scheduled as Priority 2 in the research roadmap.
