# RECENT FORM CANDIDATE DEFINITIONS

- **RF5:** Last 5 domestic matches with xG opponent residual adjustment.
- **RF10:** Last 10 domestic matches with xG opponent residual adjustment.
- **RF20:** Last 20 domestic matches.
- **RF-EWMA:** Exponentially weighted recent match performance (alpha=0.12).
- **WC-RF:** WC 2026 3-match momentum trend adapted for domestic leagues.
- **R8 + RF10:** Adaptive recency challenger combined with 10-match xG opponent residuals.
