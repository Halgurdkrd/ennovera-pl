# PRE-MATCH OPPONENT RESIDUALIZATION

Every midfield metric \(M_i\) is adjusted against pre-match opponent quality \(O_{t-1}\) and venue:
\[
	ext{Resid}(M_i) = M_i - \mathbb{E}[M \mid 	ext{Opponent\_Rating}_{t-1}, 	ext{Venue}]
\]
Ensures strong midfield performances against elite opposition receive appropriate credit.
