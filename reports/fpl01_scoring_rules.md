## 2. Squad and Starting XI Legal Constraints

- **15-Player Squad Quotas:** Exactly 2 GK, 5 DEF, 5 MID, 3 FWD.
- **Budget Limit:** Total player purchase price $\le$ £100.0m.
- **Club Limit:** Maximum 3 players from any single Premier League club.
- **Legal Formations (11 Starters):** 1 GK, minimum 3 DEF, minimum 2 MID, minimum 1 FWD (3-5-2, 3-4-3, 4-4-2, 4-3-3, 4-5-1, 5-3-2, 5-4-1, 5-2-3).
- **Captaincy Multiplier:** Selected captain scores $2\times$ points. If captain plays 0 minutes, vice-captain receives the $2\times$ multiplier.
