## 2. Captaincy Hit Distribution & Regret Analysis

- **Top-1 Hit Rate (18.5%):** In nearly 1 out of 5 Gameweeks, Ennovera's predicted top-xP player was the single highest-scoring player in the entire starting XI.
- **Top-3 Hit Rate (44.9%):** In nearly half of all Gameweeks, the chosen captain was among the top 3 scorers in the starting XI.
- **Mean Captain Regret (6.8 pts/GW):** Defined as the difference between the hindsight maximum scorer in the starting XI and the chosen captain's doubled contribution.
- **Talisman Concentration:** Over 75% of captain selections were concentrated in elite attacking talismans (Erling Haaland, Mohamed Salah, Bukayo Saka, Cole Palmer, Son Heung-min, Ollie Watkins).
