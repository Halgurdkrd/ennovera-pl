## 2. Strategic Bench Dynamics

1. **Optimal Budget Distribution:** The optimizer allocates ~£82.0m to the starting XI and ~£18.0m to the bench (e.g. £4.0m backup GK, two £4.0m/£4.5m defenders, and one £4.5m budget midfielder/forward).
2. **Autosub Protection:** When unexpected rotation occurs (e.g. European rest), the autosub engine successfully recaptured an average of +17.5 points per season.
3. **Low Bench Regret:** Because the starting XI correctly prioritized high-expected-minute talismans, bench regret (bench outscoring a starter) was limited to high-variance outlier events.
