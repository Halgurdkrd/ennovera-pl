# ENNOVERA PHASE 5.15 — SOURCE FEASIBILITY REPORT

## Evaluation of Historical Data Sources
1. **FBref / Sports-Reference:** Excellent player-level and match-level coverage for UCL, UEL, UECL, FA Cup, and League Cup. Full telemetry for minutes, starts, goals, assists, xG, xA, and shot creation. Designated **PRIMARY SOURCE**.
2. **Official UEFA Data Feeds:** Official match lineups, kickoffs, completion timestamps, and travel venues. Designated **PRIMARY SOURCE FOR MATCH CONTEXT**.
3. **The FA / EFL Feeds:** Official domestic cup fixture logs, round structures, and opponent tier metadata. Designated **SECONDARY / COMPLEMENTARY SOURCE**.
