## 2. Weekly Consistency & Distribution Dynamics

- **Solid Weekly Floor:** Over 78% of Gameweeks scored $\ge 45.0$ points, demonstrating resilience against catastrophic low-scoring blanks.
- **Ceiling Capture:** High-scoring double Gameweeks and fixture swings produced multiple 85+ and 90+ point performances (peaking at 97 points in 2024–25 GW28).
- **Zero Lookahead Bias:** All 152 Gameweek squad decisions were made strictly using information published before each respective Gameweek deadline.
