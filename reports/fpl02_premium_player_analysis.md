# ENNOVERA PL + FPL — FPL-02 Premium Player Special Analysis Report

**Research Focus:** Investigation of Premium Star Valuation (£10.0m+ Assets), Prediction Shrinkage, and Captaincy Justification.

## 1. The Premium Star Dilemma

In Fantasy Premier League, £12.5m–£15.0m players (e.g. Erling Haaland, Mohamed Salah) occupy 13–15% of total squad budget. 

Under standard linear regression (FPL-01), Haaland's expected points averaged ~6.2–6.8 pts/GW, making two £7.0m assets (each ~4.5 pts = 9.0 total) appear mathematically superior to one £14.0m asset + one £4.5m enabler. 

However, this ignores **the Captaincy Multiplier**:
- A £14.0m captain scoring 16 points delivers **32 total points**.
- Two £7.0m assets scoring 5 points each deliver **15 total points** (even if one is captained).
