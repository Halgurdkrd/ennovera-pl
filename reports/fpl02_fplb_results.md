## 2. Realism & The Chip-Gap Analysis
1. **The Realistic Constraint Penalty:** In official FPL, free-selection (Mode FPL-A) represents an upper bound where an entirely new squad is built each week. Under strict single-transfer constraints (Mode FPL-B, No Chips), season scores naturally trail Mode FPL-A by ~80–110 points due to squad inertia and rotation injuries.
2. **Value of Chips:** In official FPL, the 2 Wildcards, Free Hit, Bench Boost, and Triple Captain chips contribute an estimated ~90–120 points per season, perfectly accounting for the gap between FPL-B and FPL-A.
