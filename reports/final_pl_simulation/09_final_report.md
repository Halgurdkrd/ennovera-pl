# ENNOVERA FINAL PL 10,000-RUN SEASON SIMULATION FINAL REPORT

## Executive Summary
Using the certified, frozen `ENNOVERA_PL_FINAL_RESEARCH_V1` model, we conducted 10,000 Monte Carlo season simulations for the 2026–27 Premier League campaign under strict point-in-time constraints.

### Key Conclusions:
- **Title Race:** Manchester City (45.0%) and Arsenal (37.5%) are the leading championship contenders, followed by Liverpool (15.5%).
- **Model Evolution vs Legacy Production:** The old production simulator (V2/V5.1) severely underestimated Liverpool (1.9%) and overestimated Man City (54.4%). The final calibrated research model corrects this distortion through Dirichlet probability calibration and dynamic Bayesian strength updates.
- **Top 4 Race:** Man City (98.0%), Arsenal (97.3%), Liverpool (90.8%), and Chelsea (38.5%) form the most likely Champions League qualification tier.
