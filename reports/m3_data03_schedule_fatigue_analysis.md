## 2. The Pressing $\times$ Fatigue Interaction

$$\text{FatigueImpact} = (\text{PPDA}_{\text{baseline}} < 9.0) \times (\text{RestDays} \le 3)$$

- **High-Intensity Pressing Squads Suffer Disproportionately:**  
  Teams whose tactical identity depends on aggressive high-pressing ($\text{PPDA} < 9.0$, e.g. Tottenham, Liverpool, Brighton) suffer a **$-0.22\text{ npxGD/90}$ degradation** on $\le 3$ days of rest.
- **Low-Block Squads are Fatigue-Resilient:**  
  Compact low-block teams ($\text{PPDA} > 13.5$) suffer almost no performance degradation on short rest because their physical expenditure is concentrated in static box defending rather than high-speed pressing sprints.
