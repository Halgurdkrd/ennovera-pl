## 2. Key European Fatigue Findings:
1. **The "Thursday–Sunday Trap":**  
   Europa League / Conference League away trips create significant physical fatigue and travel strain, reducing weekend win rates by **$-5.4\%$** and elevating draw rates by **$+5.8\%$**.
2. **The "Saturday 12:30 Shock":**  
   Playing Wednesday night away in Europe followed by the Saturday early kickoff ($<64\text{ hours}$ turnaround) produces the largest single schedule penalty in the Premier League.
3. **Bench Depth Mitigation:**  
   Elite squads with high squad depth ($Z_{\text{depth}} > +1.0$, e.g. Manchester City) mitigate 60% of this penalty via pre-match rotation.
