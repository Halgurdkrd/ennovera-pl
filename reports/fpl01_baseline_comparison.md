## 2. Strategic Baseline Analysis

1. **Beating the Pure xGI Baseline:** Ennovera Component xP outperforms the standalone statistical xGI baseline (+195 cumulative points, +96 pts in 2025–26), proving that integrating clean sheet probabilities, saves, and minutes expectation is strictly superior to raw attacking rate models.
2. **The Price & Form Baseline Challenge:** In weekly free-selection mode, purely picking expensive in-form players provides a very strong benchmark. The challenge for FPL-02 will be optimizing multi-gameweek transfer planning and fixture-swing scheduling to build a decisive advantage.
3. **The Theoretical Ceiling:** The hindsight legal squad oracle scores ~153 points per Gameweek (~5,800–5,900 points per season), demonstrating massive theoretical headroom for predictive improvements.
