## 2. Cross-Task Research Mandate

From FPL-01 onward, every proposed feature engineering, model architecture, or routing modification will be simultaneously audited across:
1. **The PL Axis:** Verified all-match 1X2 accuracy, log-loss, Brier score, and strong pick accuracy.
2. **The FPL Axis:** Player xP MAE, rank correlation, weekly squad points, and captaincy performance.

No model improvement will be discarded merely because it fails one axis if it demonstrates decisive, reproducible value on the other.
