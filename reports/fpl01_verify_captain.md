## 2. Why Does the Price Rule Outperform Mean xP for Captaincy?

1. **The Asymmetry of the Captaincy Multiplier:** In Fantasy Premier League, captain points are doubled ($2\times$). An elite £14.0m forward who has a 25% chance of a 16-point haul and a 40% chance of a 2-point blank delivers a massive upside when captained ($+16$ extra points on hauls), which outweighs a steady 5-point mid-priced asset.
2. **Mean Prediction vs Tail Upside:** Ennovera's component regression estimates the expected mean, which naturally shrinks extreme haul probabilities toward the average. The Price rule acts as a reliable proxy for raw attacking ceiling and explosive potential.
